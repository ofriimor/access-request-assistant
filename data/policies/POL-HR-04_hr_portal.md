# HR Portal Access Policy

The HR portal holds employee records, salaries and performance reviews.

- Only HR and Management may access employee records, read-only through this process.
- Every employee can see their own payslip through the self-service app;
  that does not require an access request.
- Maximum duration is 90 days; default is 30 days.
